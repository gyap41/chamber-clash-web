Use case: stylized-concept.
ONE landscape 2x2 comparison concept art board for a top-down 2D action roguelike. Four views of EXACTLY THE SAME rectangular ruined foundry chamber, identical dimensions, camera, floor tile size, wall height, light positions and four central doorways. The only difference is pillar layout. Labels in clean readable type: "01 OPEN", "02 CENTRAL", "03 FOUR PILLARS", "04 ASYMMETRIC". Broad gutters between panels. Strict orthographic strong overhead view, screen-horizontal north wall, not isometric, no vanishing point. Strong readable stone pillar top planes, short visible front faces, contact shadows falling consistently downward. Ancient weathered ash-grey stone, subtle brass bands, small amber wall sconces, restrained soot and cracks, dark exterior void. Rich pixel-art-inspired game environment detail, subdued floor grid and atmospheric texture, gameplay legibility. Clearly upright architectural columns, not flat floor blocks. Pillars should be architectural cutaways with consistent visible height about 1.5 character heights, top-dominant perspective, no roof.
01: open chamber with ZERO freestanding pillars.
02: exactly ONE large square stone pillar in center, about twice the footprint width of the smaller columns in 03, leaving generous circulation around all four sides.
03: exactly FOUR identical medium square stone columns at quarter points forming a rectangle, leaving a large open central fighting space and wide edge circulation. Not against walls.
04: exactly THREE medium square stone columns staggered asymmetrically: one upper-left, one slightly right of center, one lower-left. Well spaced to offer varied lines of sight, no narrow choke.
Each panel has ONE identical tiny adventurer scale-reference character, ochre jacket and dark trousers, at the same lower-center position, about 1/9 room height; never change character size. No other characters, no enemies. Pillar widths approximately 1.5 character widths for the smaller columns. Doorways on all four sides kept unobstructed. Leave enough floor width for dodging and large enemies. No additional chests/crates/machinery that could be mistaken for extra pillars. No arrows, no collision circles, no UI bars, no prose. Top header "PILLAR LAYOUT STUDIES". A polished game concept board, full rooms visible, no crop.
