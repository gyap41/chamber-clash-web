Mock gameplay screen (no UI, no text) for a 2D top-down action game: one standard room of an old ash-covered foundry, used to fix the environment art style.

Reference image 1 is the player "Rina" (STYLE MASTER). Reference image 2 is the approved style sheet: its floor swatch, pillar, anvil and crate show how the environment must be drawn. Reference image 3 is an enemy already drawn in this style. Draw EVERYTHING with the same technique: thick dark brown ink outlines, flat colours with 2-3 cel-shading steps, light from the upper left, big simple shapes, no painterly texture, no photographic stone, no noise.

Environment rule: the background keeps its ink lines but is calmer than the characters: duller, darker, lower contrast, lower saturation, fewer and larger shapes. Characters and enemies must be the brightest and most colourful things on screen.

View: strong top-down 3/4 view (about 70 degrees from the ground), orthographic, horizontal edges stay horizontal, no perspective convergence, no isometric angle.

Room layout:
- North wall across the whole top of the image: a flat stone wall-top cap seen from above, and below it the wall's front face of large stone blocks, about half as tall as Rina. A doorway gap through the north wall right of centre, dark inside.
- West wall down the left edge: we see only its flat stone top from above. At the top-left corner the west wall's top joins the north wall's top continuously (no pillar, no seam, no frame).
- Floor: large irregular stone slabs, each slab about as long as Rina is tall, separated by dark ink joint lines, a few simple cracks, dark warm grey with a hint of brown. It is the darkest and calmest area.
- Walls use the same stone as the floor, slightly lighter, with an ink line only on their outer edges.
- Against the north wall, left of the door: a squat stone-and-iron furnace with a glowing orange mouth, a little ash and soot on the floor in front of it only.
- Against the north wall, right of the door: a wooden workbench seen mostly from the top (tabletop dominant, short front, legs hidden) with a few tools.
- One iron wall lamp mounted on the north wall face.
- On the floor: one square stone pillar (upper right), an iron anvil on a low wooden block (middle right), a steel-banded wooden crate and a pallet (lower left).
- Leave a wide open floor area in the middle for fighting.

Characters for scale: Rina near the centre facing down-right with her large pistol, about 9 percent of the image height. The green fire-pouch lizard from reference 3 in the lower right, crawling toward her, its throat pouch glowing orange. Soft dark oval shadows under all figures and props.
