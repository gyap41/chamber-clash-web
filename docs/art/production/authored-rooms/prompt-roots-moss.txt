Sprite sheet of six separate flat root and moss pieces for a 2D top-down action game: ancient underground stone ruins overgrown by tree roots.

Reference image 1 is an approved sheet of floor decorations and roots of this set, and reference image 2 an approved sheet with a root trunk: match their drawing technique, root colours and line weight exactly (thick dark brown ink outlines, flat 2-3 step cel shading, light from the upper left, big simple shapes, no painterly texture). Reference image 3 is the stone floor.

All pieces lie on or in the floor, seen from above. Keep the moss muted: dull olive and grey-green, lower saturation than the reference moss.

Layout: exactly 3 columns and 2 rows, one piece per cell, centred with generous margin, never touching another cell. Solid flat pure magenta background (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no labels, no text, no cast shadows.

Row 1, left to right:
1. SUNKEN ROOT, LONG: one thick root half buried in the floor, running left to right in a gentle S curve, flagstones around it cracked and lifted along its sides, small rootlets only along its length (NOT leg-like splayed roots at the ends), both ends disappearing into the floor, about 4 times wider than tall.
2. SUNKEN ROOT, FORKED: one thick root half buried in the floor that splits into two branches, cracked lifted flagstones along it, ends disappearing into the floor, about 2.5 times wider than tall.
3. MOSS, LONG: an elongated irregular strip of muted moss creeping along the floor, ragged edges, about 3 times wider than tall.

Row 2, left to right:
4. MOSS, CLUMPS: four or five small separate muted moss clumps scattered together.
5. MOSS IN CRACKS: thin lines of moss growing in the joints between flagstones, forming a partial grid pattern, roughly square.
