Sprite sheet of six separate flat floor overlays for a 2D top-down action game: ancient underground stone ruins with an old foundry. They blend objects and walls into the stone floor.

Reference image 1 is the stone floor they lie on (warm grey-brown flagstones with dark brown ink joints). Reference image 2 is an approved sheet of floor decorations of the same set: match its drawing technique exactly (thick dark brown ink outlines where needed, flat 2-3 step cel shading, big simple shapes, no painterly texture, no fine noise). Reference image 3 is the props sheet of the same set.

All overlays lie flat on the floor, seen straight from above, with soft irregular edges, subtle and calm: only a little darker or lighter than the floor, muted earthy colours. No cast shadows.

Layout: exactly 3 columns and 2 rows, one overlay per cell, centred with generous margin, never touching another cell. Solid flat pure magenta background (#FF00FF) everywhere. No floor, no labels, no text.
